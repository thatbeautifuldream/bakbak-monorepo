## Design Context

### Users

People reading articles and other web content who want to listen instead of read. They use the browser extension in the moment, without leaving the page, to start a clear and comfortable narration.

### Brand Personality

Polished, friendly, and focused. The experience should feel dependable and welcoming while keeping the user’s attention on the page they chose to hear.

### Aesthetic Direction

A compact, restrained floating player that works cleanly in light and dark environments. It should use calm surfaces, clear type, and one obvious primary action; the extension should never expose implementation details such as local development URLs.

### Design Principles

1. Keep actions and status understandable at a glance.
2. Make required next steps explicit and effortless.
3. Use friendly, concise language without technical jargon.
4. Preserve focus by keeping the interface compact and visually quiet.
5. Support keyboard users and reduced-motion preferences as first-class needs.
