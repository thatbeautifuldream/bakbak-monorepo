## Design Context

### Users

People browsing the web who want a conversational companion without leaving the page. They use the browser extension in the moment to ask questions, understand context, and find details in the page they are viewing.

### Brand Personality

Polished, friendly, and focused. The experience should feel dependable and welcoming while keeping the user’s attention on the page they chose to hear.

### Aesthetic Direction

A compact, expressive floating conversation surface that works cleanly in light and dark environments. It should use warm paper-like surfaces, clear type, and one obvious primary action; the extension should never expose implementation details such as local development URLs.

### Design Principles

1. Keep actions and status understandable at a glance.
2. Make required next steps explicit and effortless.
3. Use friendly, concise language without technical jargon.
4. Preserve focus by keeping the interface compact and visually quiet.
5. Support keyboard users and reduced-motion preferences as first-class needs.
